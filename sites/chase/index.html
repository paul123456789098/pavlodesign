FAQ

Answers to common questions about Causa Prima.

Is Causa Prima secure?

Secure by design. Causa Prima is the easiest part of your security review.

  • SAPcertified
  • ISO 27001ongoing
  • SOC 2 Type 2ongoing
  • GDPRcompliant
How is this different from the tools we already have?

Your current tools only see your side of the transaction. But disputes, delays and missed discounts happen between companies. Causa Prima brings your suppliers and customers onto the same network, so agents on both sides see live data from each company and resolve issues directly.

Who’s the agent on the other side?

If they’re already on the network, it’s your customer’s or supplier’s agent, running their rules, representing their side. If they’re not, our agent reaches out to them directly, by email, the same way a person on your team would today, so nothing changes on their end.

Does this replace what we already run?

No, we sit on top of what you run: SAP-certified, wrapping existing tools rather than replacing them. Nobody re-learns their job and no system gets ripped out.

How do agents make decisions?

It follows the rules you set, shows its reasoning, and flags anything it isn’t sure about instead of guessing. The system is secure by design, so every action is authorized, logged, and auditable. You decide what it does unsupervised.
